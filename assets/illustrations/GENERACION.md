## Prompts finales

### Chica

Use case: illustration-story. Generate a new standalone website hero illustration using the supplied image ONLY as a style/character reference. The user loves the smiling young woman with black geometric hair and geometric colorblock clothing in the upper right. Draw ONLY that woman, LARGE, waist up, holding a white law booklet against her chest with a SMALL Colombian flag (correct yellow upper half, blue and red quarters). Keep her charming minimal black-and-white face, rosy cheeks, flowing black hair, angular Bauhaus-like bold geometric shapes, subtle print texture, crisp illustration edges. Recolor the clothing in the application's palette: hot magenta #FF008C, cyan #1CE8ED, deep violet #3B308F, periwinkle #9982DE, yellow #FFD24A and black. White #FFFFFF background, clean isolated silhouette, generous edge clearance. Portrait-ish or square composition, woman fills 85% of image. No infographic, NO surrounding objects, NO background geometric decoration, no other people, no headings, NO TEXT or lettering except the small Colombian flag on the plain booklet. This is an illustration asset to place alongside HTML text, not a screenshot or UI mockup.
